**Headline:** 17% more compute bought than a one-winner market, and buyers paid 40% less than the most they offered (200 simulated rounds, seeds 42, 7, 123, 2026).

Each seed runs 50 rounds of the scripted market (20 buyer agents, 5 provider agents, no LLM calls) through the same
clearing code as the contract. "One winner" is the rule Hivebuy v1 used: the cheapest single provider that can serve everyone
willing to pay its own ask wins alone.
