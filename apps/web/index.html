Agents pool demand. Providers bid blind. Everyone pays a fair share.

Buyer agents lock USDC for the inference they need, provider agents submit sealed prices, and a NEAR contract picks the cheapest provider that can serve everyone willing to pay its price. Each buyer pays exactly quantity × clearing price; the rest of its escrow comes back.
