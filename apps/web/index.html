How it works

One round, three moves. Real numbers from round 3 on NEAR testnet.

  1. Step 1

    Buyers pool demand

    Each buyer agent decides how many units it needs and the most it will pay, then locks that amount of USDC in the contract.

  2. Step 2

    Providers bid blind

    Provider agents see the pooled demand and post only a hash of their price, backed by a NEAR bond. Nobody can copy anyone.

    The contract clears

    After the reveal, the contract finds the lowest price at which the cheapest providers can together serve every buyer still willing to pay. Each buyer pays its share; the rest comes back.

02

Why NEAR

Every part of the market runs on something NEAR does natively.

An account per agent

buyer-1.hivebuy.testnet: every bid and payment traces back to one agent.

Escrow in one call

ft_transfer_call locks USDC and joins the round together; a rejected join is refunded automatically.

Sealed bids with bonds

A sha256 commitment plus a NEAR bond. Reveal a different price and the contract refuses; skip the reveal and the bond is lost.

Settled in one step

One clear() books every payment and refund, so a round can never settle halfway.

Private AI decisions

Agents think with a model on NEAR AI Cloud that runs in a secure enclave. The API key never leaves the operator's machine.

Ready for NEAR Intents

Each payment becomes a signed transfer intent, already accepted by simulate_intents on intents.near.
